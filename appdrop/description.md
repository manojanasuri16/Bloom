**Bloom is a private, beautiful cycle tracker that never leaves your phone.**

No account. No cloud. No analytics. No ads. Bloom doesn't even have the internet permission — it is technically unable to send your data anywhere.

### What you get
- **Honest predictions** — your next period is shown as a window with a confidence level, not a false-precision date. Fertile window and ovulation are clearly labelled estimates.
- **Effortless daily logging** — flow in one tap; symptoms, mood, discharge, intimacy, temperature and notes in a quick sheet that saves as you go.
- **Calendar, insights & history** — phase-coloured month view, cycle-length trends, symptom and mood patterns, temperature chart.
- **Widgets in three sizes** — small ring, medium with a 7-day strip, large with a two-week view. They update by themselves, and a *Discreet* mode shows only a neutral dot.
- **Real privacy** — AES-256 encryption with a hardware-backed key, optional PIN and biometric lock, optional screenshot blocking, OS backups disabled.
- **Your data, portable** — complete JSON export, CSV export, password-encrypted backups, and restore with merge or replace.
- Light and dark themes, optional neutral reminders.

*Bloom's predictions are statistical estimates, not medical advice, and must not be used for contraception.*
